Letter to the Editor

Dear Editor,

We thank the reviewer for an exceptionally careful and constructive report. Its central criticism is correct, and we have acted on it. Our earlier manuscript analysed what the technique adjudicates in the regime the technique exists for, but left the detection question in that regime unanswered; the ablation table's pooled numbers then let the metamorphic family look competitive for the wrong reason. That gap is now closed by a new analysis (Section 4.3 and Table 2), and the effect is to make our claims sharper rather than wider: the new stratum analysis strengthens Finding 2 where its antecedent holds, and, where it does not, quantifies the relation's value as exactly what the crawl-derived reachability model can see. We have also acted on every other major and minor point: the formal core is reframed (Proposition 1), the conclusion is priced honestly, the paper now carries three figures, the abstract has been rewritten to the reviewer's suggested shape, repeated scope statements have been consolidated, and the missing related work is now discussed.

The manuscript has grown from 13 to 16 pages. The growth is almost entirely the new stratum analysis, the three figures the reviewer asked for, and the added related-work discussion. Every number remains generated by the analysis pipeline from the raw records; none is hand-entered.

We are grateful for a report that made the paper materially stronger and more honest.

Sincerely, Qiulong Lin, Nanlei Chen

Response to major concerns

M1. The formal core of F2 and F3 is trivial; the real contribution must be argued more explicitly

The reviewer notes that "V = f(O,R) equals D := f(O,R)" is a tautology, and that the proposition with actual empirical content is "the adjudication of the evaluated MR family under a complete oracle is a function of (O,R)"—an empirical premise established by source inspection and experiment, which the formal wrapper concealed. The reviewer asks that Lemma 1 be rewritten with that premise as its subject, and that F3's emphasis move from "we prove a dilution property" to "we identify a specific failure mode of a published metric (99.81%)."

We accept both points and have implemented them.

(a) Proposition 1 (formerly Lemma 1) is rewritten so that its subject is the empirical premise, not the algebraic identity. It now reads (abridged): for the authorization relations and scenarios evaluated here—whose predicates read the crawl-time reachability model M and the response, and nothing else—once a readable, sound, complete oracle O is supplied, M contributes no decision-relevant authorization information that (O,R) does not already determine: agreement is redundant, disagreement is unsound (Finding 1). The verdict of the metamorphic family is therefore a function f(O,R), and any adjudicator of that form is extensionally equal to the direct consumer D := f(O,R). The first clause is stated as the premise and is established by source inspection (§7) and the ablation (§4.4); only the residual "a function agrees pointwise with itself" remains algebraic, and it is now a subordinate step rather than the headline. The environment is renamed from lemma to proposition throughout, and the surrounding text no longer presents the result as a discovered effect. We have kept the strict scope: the proposition concerns adjudication information only, and explicitly does not deny metamorphic testing a role in input generation, search-space reduction, or execution efficiency.

(b) F3 is reframed around the published metric. The emphasis has moved from the arithmetic of the denominator to the concrete diagnostic claim about the published figure: the reported specificity of 99.81% is not conditioned on an explicit check that the reachability model equals the authorization state, and its input-level denominator cannot register a class-uniform failure. The elementary dilution is retained only as the precise second half of the diagnosis, and the text now says so ("offered only to make the second half of the diagnosis precise; we do not present it as the contribution"). The Finding 3 subsection heading, the introduction's third contribution, and the conclusion have all been adjusted accordingly.

M2. (Most important) The v1/v2 recall (0.92/0.99) versus direct_observe recall (0.50) asymmetry is not addressed

The reviewer observes that the "no incremental benefit" conclusion holds only for v3 vs. direct_observe, while v2—the closest to the published technique's actual behaviour—has twice direct_observe's recall; that the MR step's entire incremental value therefore lies in the oracle-absent stratum, the very regime metamorphic testing exists for; and that the paper analysed only adjudication there, never detection. The reviewer asks for a detection analysis of the oracle-absent stratum (precision/recall of v1/v2 against a no-oracle baseline), and observes that F1+F2 jointly imply a stronger, more pointed conclusion for the black-box case that the paper only skirts.

This was the decisive point, and it is now the heart of the revision.

(a) The asymmetry was a pooling artefact, and we show it. We added Section 4.3 and Table 2, which re-run the identical comparison inside each stratum of effective oracle readability. One disclosure is necessary here and is now made in the text (§4.3 and §7): we had initially stratified on the configuration switch (introspection=on/off), and our own consistency checks caught that this is not a faithful proxy—108 of the records flagged introspection=on nevertheless returned no reading (the two no-exception configurations). The stratifying variable is therefore the value the probe actually returns, not the flag. The two strata partition all 576 records as 312 readable and 264 unreadable.

(b) In the readable stratum, the split strengthens Finding 2 rather than rescuing the family. On the 312 readable records, direct_observe reaches recall 1.00 with 0 false proofs, against v2's recall 0.97 with 36 false proofs (v3: 0.97 with 0). The plain request is ahead on false proofs and not behind on detections, so the pooled table had let the family appear competitive on recall only because it mixed in records where the oracle-bearing methods could not run. We still do not claim domination: direct_observe is built from the same two quantities that define the reference, and the comparison rests on the constructed-observable argument of Proposition 1.

(c) The unreadable stratum is the relation's own regime, and we now characterise it. The 264 unreadable records contain 108 true violations and 156 correct cases—a base rate of 40.91%. Every oracle-bearing method (v3, direct_observe, direct_only) detects none of them, by definition, because there is nothing to consult. We therefore score the metamorphic family against two references that need no oracle: the trivial always_alarm floor and obs_success (a single uninterpreted request). v2 reaches all 108 violations (recall 1.00) at precision 0.82—a lift of 2.00× over the base rate; obs_success reaches the same recall at precision 0.69 (lift 1.69×); always_alarm has precision equal to the base rate itself, i.e. no information. The entire difference between the metamorphic family and obs_success is therefore not in detections but in false alarms: ΔTP = 0, ΔFP = −24.

(d) And the saving is fully attributable. All 24 suppressed false alarms fall on S3—the legitimate share created through the framework's own facility and hence recorded in its log; on S6, the out-of-band share, the two methods agree exactly (24 vs. 24). So the incremental detection information the metamorphic step carries where no oracle exists is exactly the information the crawl-derived reachability model can see: it suppresses what was logged, and on what was not logged it reproduces Finding 1. We therefore bound Finding 2 rather than extend it. The text states the two limitations explicitly: the oracle-free references are deliberately weak (a differential multi-account prober was not instantiated, so the lift must not be read as a comparison against the best oracle-free detector), and both share an observable with the labelling rule, so only the differences against them are meant to be read.

This directly answers the reviewer's question Q1 (see below).

(e) The joint corollary of F1 and F2 is now stated, not skirted (reviewer's point 1). We have added the inference the reviewer identifies, in §6.2: read together, the two findings bound what the technique's adjudication can yield for the proposition studied—if the reachability stand-in is the only warrant and is wrong, a legitimate access becomes a false proof (F1); if a readable, sound oracle is available, the relation adds no adjudication over the direct consumer (F2); and where no readable, admissible oracle exists—the black-box case—there is nothing for a sound method to consume, so it abstains rather than proves. Across the statuses the oracle can take, the outcomes the evaluated relations can yield are a false proof, a tie, or an abstention; adjudication of a genuinely illegal access is not among them for these relations. We accept the reviewer's reading and say so explicitly.

M3. The gap between the generalisation ceiling and the actual significance needs honest pricing

Only two MRs of one "missing-evidence-gated" family; the native run fires 2 of 11 authorization relations and never invokes the content predicate; the real-system side tests only the false-proof face; the 40% Gitea rate comes from a self-designed topology. The reviewer asks that the Conclusion price this and add a "what a practitioner should do differently" paragraph.

Done in the Conclusion (§9).

(a) Three stacked ceilings, stated as such. The conclusion now states, in one place, that (i) our two relations are two of a catalogue of 76 and both belong to one reachability-gated family; (ii) even the execution of the published relations reaches only that family—2 of 11 authorization relations fire (OTG_AUTHZ_002, OTG_AUTHZ_002c) and userCanRetrieveContent is never invoked (0 calls)—so that evidence is bounded the same way; and (iii) the real-system study exercises the false-proof face on a single constructed fault pattern, not the detection face against a population of real flaws, and the Gitea channel proportions are topology design parameters, not field prevalence. Together these "fix the ceiling on what a practitioner may transfer."

(b) A new paragraph, "What a practitioner should do differently," gives three operational consequences, each bounded by what was measured: do not read a reachability-gated relation's alarm as proof; if a readable, sound oracle is available, consult it directly and drop the relation (for the relations studied it adds no adjudication information once that antecedent holds); and treat a reported input-level specificity as a corpus statistic rather than a guarantee of scenario-level resolution. The paragraph closes by locating the technique's contribution where no oracle exists, and bounding it by what the reachability reading can see.

All five are now cited and discussed in §8 (Related work).

Response to the reviewer's questions

Q1. Within the oracle-absent stratum, what is the detection gain of v1/v2 alarms over any no-oracle baseline? This is now answered by §4.3 and Table 2. In the 264-record unreadable stratum (108 true violations, 40.91% base rate), v2 reaches all 108 violations at precision 0.82 (lift 2.00× over the base rate), against a single uninterpreted request (obs_success) at recall 1.00 / precision 0.69 (lift 1.69×) and the trivial always_alarm floor at precision equal to the base rate. The difference between the metamorphic family and obs_success is entirely in false alarms (ΔTP = 0, ΔFP = −24), and all 24 suppressed alarms fall on the in-band scenario (S3) recorded in the framework's log; on the out-of-band scenario (S6) the two agree exactly. The gain is therefore real but bounded: it is exactly what the crawl-derived reachability model can see.

Q2. Do you accept the joint corollary of F1 and F2 for the black-box case—that the technique must either false-proof or abstain, and cannot adjudicate? If so, why not state it? We accept the reading, and it is now stated explicitly in §6.2 rather than skirted. For the authorization proposition studied, the outcomes the evaluated relations can yield across the oracle's possible statuses are a false proof, a tie, or an abstention; adjudication of a genuinely illegal access is not among them for these relations. We had held the statement back out of a concern not to over-claim beyond the evaluated relations; the reviewer is right that withholding it made the paper less clear without making it safer, so it is now in the text with the same explicit scope.

Q3. Do you object to rewriting Lemma 1 as a proposition whose subject is the empirical premise? No objection—we agree, and it is done. Lemma 1 is now Proposition 1, with the empirical premise ("the evaluated family's adjudication reduces to (O,R) once a readable, sound, complete oracle is supplied") as its subject; the algebraic step is subordinate.

Q4. In the native run, userCanRetrieveContent is invoked zero times and only 2/11 relations fire. Does that itself constitute a further (unclaimed) finding about MST-wi's engineering usability? We report the fact prominently and treat it as a bound on our own evidence, not as a finding about the technique. The revision states it in the Conclusion's ceiling paragraph (2 of 11 relations fire; the content predicate is never invoked). We deliberately stop short of claiming an engineering-usability defect from it, because the harness, not the technique, decides which relations are exercised in one run, and our gate family is a subset; asserting more would exceed what a single execution licenses.

Q5. On the three real systems, no real vulnerability was found. Did you try injecting configuration-level authorization defects (rather than only the self-built target) to close the detection face on real systems? We did not inject authorization defects into the three production systems. The seeded positive control (§7) plants a defect on a constructed vulnerable-by-design target—same fault pattern on one route, correctly not treated as independent defects—and closes the loop for one pattern (all planted instances detected, zero false positives), which is why we call it a sanity check and not validation. Injecting configuration-level defects into live Gitea/Gogs/GitLab instances was out of scope for this study and would change the systems under test; we state this limit explicitly and reserve the detection face against a population of real flaws as future work, alongside relations whose gate is not the absence of evidence.

Closing

We thank the reviewer again. The report's central observation—that the ablation's pooled numbers obscured the very question the technique exists to answer—was correct, and closing it has changed the paper for the better: Finding 2 is now bounded rather than asserted, its surviving value is quantified and attributed, and the joint corollary is stated plainly. We hope the revision meets the bar for acceptance.
