# M2 补充分析：oracle 缺失层的检测增益

完成时间 2026-09-20 · 针对审稿意见 **M2**（"v2 recall 0.99 vs direct_observe 0.50 的不对称未被处理；oracle 缺失层的检测分析缺失"）

## 1. 结论先行

分层后，**两个方向都被证实**，而且比原先的池化叙述更有力：

- **可读层（312 条）→ Finding 2 被加强**，不是仅仅"无增量收益"：`direct_observe` 误报更少（0 vs v2 的 36）且不漏检更多（recall 1.00 vs 0.97）。原先池化表让 MR 家族只在 recall 上显得有竞争力。
- **缺失层（264 条）→ MR 家族是唯一能跑的检测器**，但它相对"无 oracle 基线"的增量**恰好等于爬取模型能看到的那部分**：全部省下的误报都落在 S3（带内共享，框架日志有记录），对 S6（带外共享）与朴素基线**报得一模一样**。
- 因此 **pooled recall 0.99 vs 0.50 是一个池化假象**：`direct_observe` 在可读层是 1.00、在缺失层是 0.00（无从行动），而 v2 两层都能行动（0.97 / 1.00）。

一句话回答审稿人：**Finding 2 的前件（可读、可靠、完备的 oracle）在缺失层不成立；在前件成立的那一层，结论比原来更强；在前件不成立的那一层，MR 的价值被量化为"爬取模型视野所及的部分"，并且恰好以 Finding 1 的假证明为代价。**

### ★ 一个必须披露的口径修正

最初按 `config.introspection`（on/off）分层，被脚本自身的校验拦下：**introspection=on 的记录里有 108 条 probe 实返 `unavailable`**。追查确认是 `V-noE3-vuln0/vuln1` 两个配置——config 标记开启了自省，但 probe 实际没有读数。

→ **配置开关不是 oracle 可读性的忠实代理**。决定 `direct_*` 能否行动的变量是 probe 返回值，故分层变量改为**有效可读性**。此披露已写入正文 §4.3 与 §7 Threats。

## 5. 可直接粘贴用的回复审稿人措辞（English）

> **R1/M2.** The asymmetry is real and it was a pooling artefact; we have added the analysis the reviewer asks for (new Section 4.3, Table 2). The records partition by one variable — whether the introspection surface returned a reading for the record — and the two strata behave in opposite ways, so the pooled table was misleading in both directions. Where the oracle is readable (312 records), direct\_observe returns 108/108 true violations with 0 false proofs against v2's 105/108 and 36 false proofs: in the regime Finding 2 describes, the conclusion is stronger than "no observed incremental benefit", and pooling had let the relation look competitive on recall. Where the oracle is unreadable (264 records, 108 true violations), the antecedent fails — v3, direct\_only and direct\_observe detect nothing by construction, because there is nothing to consult — and we therefore compare the metamorphic family against two oracle-free references scored on the same records: v2 reaches all 108 violations at precision 0.82 (lift 2.0× over a 40.9% base rate) while a single uninterpreted request reaches the same recall at precision 0.69 (lift 1.69×) and the trivial always-alarm floor at precision equal to the base rate. The entire difference is attributable: all 24 suppressed alarms fall on the scenario in which the legitimate share is recorded in the framework's own log (S3), and on the out-of-band share (S6) the two agree exactly — the relation retains exactly what the crawl-derived reachability model can see, and reproduces Finding 1 on what it cannot. We therefore bound Finding 2 rather than extend it, and we do not claim the relation is worthless outside its antecedent. Two limitations are stated in the text: the oracle-free references are deliberately weak (a differential multi-account prober was not instantiated, so this is not a comparison against the best oracle-free detector), and they share an observable with the labelling rule, so only the differences against them — not their absolute rates — are to be read. We have also disclosed that the configuration flag is not a faithful proxy for oracle readability (108 records in introspection-enabled configurations return no reading), so stratification uses the returned reading.
