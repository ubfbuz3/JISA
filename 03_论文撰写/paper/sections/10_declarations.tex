\section*{Declarations}

\subsection*{Data availability}

The raw records of the studies reported here are publicly available in the
companion repository \url{https://github.com/ubfbuz3/JISA}, version-pinned by the
tag \texttt{access-resubmission-v11}, which archives every record behind every
number in this paper---including the two experiments added in this revision, the
matched-budget sweep (\texttt{results\allowbreak/baseline\_budget.json}) and the
four-class defect study
(\texttt{real\_system\allowbreak/results\allowbreak/seeded\_multiclass.json})---the
frozen admission protocol, its errata and the application register
(\texttt{real\_system\allowbreak/prereg/}), and the generation scripts that
recompute the tables from those records.
The earlier tags remain in place as snapshots of prior states and should not be
conflated with it: \texttt{artifact-v1} is the previously reviewed version
(without the temporal or vulnerable/fixed records added here),
\texttt{access-resubmission-v2} is the working freeze at which an internal
accuracy review was started, \texttt{access-resubmission-v3} is the next review
snapshot, \texttt{access-resubmission-v4} includes the subsequent corrections,
and \texttt{access-resubmission-v9} is the freeze two steps back, which contains
initial runs of both experiments but not the corrected execution order of the
budget sweep, the per-cell request counts or the per-channel negative controls
reported here; \texttt{access-resubmission-v10} is the intermediate freeze those
corrections were made against.
The revision adds, under
\texttt{experiments/}:
(1)~the scenario/configuration study
(\texttt{results/raw\_matrix.jsonl}, \texttt{ablation\_direct*.json},
\texttt{alt\_matrix.jsonl});
(2)~the real-system cell study across Gitea~\ResRsVersion{}, Gogs~\GgVersion{} and
GitLab~\GlVersion{} (\texttt{real\_system\allowbreak/results/*.json});
(3)~the temporal loop and its archived pilot, with the protocol and both
amendments by digest (\texttt{real\_system\allowbreak/results\allowbreak/temporal/},
\texttt{c\_protocol*.json}); (4)~the real vulnerable/fixed pair
(\texttt{real\_system\allowbreak/results\allowbreak/cve\_pair.json});
(5)~the execution of the published relations on the native engine
(\texttt{native\_engine\allowbreak/results/*.json});
and (6)~the seeded positive control (\texttt{seeded.json}).
The two records added in this revision sit beside them:
(7)~the equal-budget sweep with its per-cell records
(\texttt{results\allowbreak/baseline\_budget.json}) and
(8)~the four-class defect study with per-instance and per-channel negative
records (\texttt{real\_system\allowbreak/results\allowbreak/seeded\_multiclass.json}).
The analysis pipeline (\texttt{analysis/*.py}) recomputes every number, table and
figure from those records, including the design audit
(\texttt{ingest\_design.py}), the decision-scored coverage table
(\texttt{ingest\_coverage.py}) and the truth table (\texttt{ingest\_truth.py});
\texttt{check\_macro\_use.py} asserts that every macro cited in the manuscript
has exactly one definition. \texttt{bash reproduce.sh} regenerates all outputs
and the PDF, and \texttt{bash verify\_artifact.sh} checks the SHA-256 of every raw
record and of every byte-reproducible output. The manuscript \LaTeX{} source ships
in the same repository. The DOI \url{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22859942}
archives the previously reviewed version; a new Zenodo version covering this
revision will be deposited at acceptance, and the git tag above is the
version-pinned copy in the meantime.

Not applicable. This study involved no human participants, no personal data, and
no animals. All experiments were performed against software systems that we
deployed and controlled locally.

\section*{Declaration of generative AI and AI-assisted technologies in the writing process}

During the preparation of this work the authors used Claude (Anthropic) to assist
with literature verification, the development of the analysis scripts, and
language editing. Where it contributed to analysis code, every generated or
modified script was reviewed, executed, and its output checked by the authors
against the raw records. After using this tool, the authors reviewed and edited
the content as needed and take full responsibility for the content of the
published article. All experimental computations and final reported values were
independently checked by the authors against the released scripts and raw
records.
