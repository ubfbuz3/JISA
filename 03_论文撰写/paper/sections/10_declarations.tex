\section*{Declarations}

\subsection*{Data availability}

The raw records of the three studies reported here---the factorial study, the
real-system cell study, and the execution of the published relations---together
with the scripts that compute every number in this paper from those records, were
produced by the analysis pipeline described in
Section~\ref{sec:protocol}. They are available from the corresponding author on
request.

\subsection*{Competing interests}

The author declares no competing interests, financial or otherwise.

\subsection*{Funding}

This research received no specific grant from any funding agency in the public,
commercial, or not-for-profit sectors.

\subsection*{Authors' contributions}

Both authors contributed to the work. Qiulong Lin and Nanlei Chen jointly
developed the conceptualisation and methodology, designed and implemented the
software and analysis pipeline, carried out the investigation and formal
analysis, and wrote the manuscript.

\subsection*{Ethics}

Not applicable. This study involved no human participants, no personal data, and
no animals. All experiments were performed against software systems that we
deployed and controlled locally.
