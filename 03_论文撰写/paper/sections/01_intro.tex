\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

\PARstart{B}{roken} object-level authorization (BOLA), first in the OWASP API
Security Top~10~\cite{OWASP2023API1}, is the failure to enforce, per object, that the
authenticated subject is entitled to the object touched. Detecting it is an
\emph{oracle} problem: vulnerable and correct systems answer with the same success
status. Metamorphic testing is an established approach to that oracle problem, and
\mbox{MST-wi}~\cite{Chaleshtari2022Metamorphic} is a substantial system-agnostic
instance for Web systems: \ResLitMrCatalog{} system-agnostic relations (MRs) over
\ResLitSmrlFunctions{} Web-specific predicates, reported to detect
\ResLitReportedDetection{}\% of vulnerabilities with specificity
\ResLitReportedSpecPct{}\%.

This paper asks what happens to that claim when the asserted property is
object-level authorization. The predicates do not express entitlement; they express
what their authors call reachability---what ``the functionality exposed by the user
interface'' permits---with the role-to-resource mapping ``\textbf{not always
available}''~\cite{Chaleshtari2022Metamorphic}. Delegating adjudication to a
reachability model makes the moment the model was read part of the specification, so
we instantiate the predicates \emph{as defined}---over a model recorded at crawl
time---and drive the real systems through three phases: crawl, change authorization
out of band, execute. The middle phase is the one the original evaluation does not
exercise. The constructed study isolates the same defect without time: it seeds a
share outside the framework's record and asks whether a gate that cannot see it
still calls the access legal. We ran a \ResSynRecords{}-record
scenario/configuration study on a constructed system and a controlled study on Gitea
\ResRsVersion{}, extended, with a methodological variation, to Gogs \GgVersion{} and
GitLab \GlVersion{}. We propose no mechanism; three findings survive, one per
research question (Section~\ref{sec:findings}).

\paragraph{Research questions and findings}
(1)~\emph{Temporal (RQ1).} Does the crawl-time reachability model remain sound for
authorization decisions after an out-of-band authorization change? No: the
adjudication as designed produces \emph{deterministic false proofs}---a legitimate
access the model cannot see is reported as a proven violation once an alarm is
promoted, at rate \ResObsOobVtwoFpRate{} in the constructed study and
\ResRsMrBookkeepingFpRate{} on Gitea. The predicate's two representations cannot
describe either grant, so the error is a property of the \emph{reading}, not of the
system. The alarm and the count of proven violations are separable: no policy
changes what the relations return, while the number of contradictable claims runs
from none, through \PolPallFalse{} under promote-every-alarm, to \PolPoracleFalse{}
under the oracle-gated variant (Table~\ref{tab:promotion}).
(2)~\emph{Oracle (RQ2).} When a readable, sound authorization oracle fully resolves
the proposition under test, does the metamorphic step add incremental adjudication
information beyond the oracle and the request outcome? Not on the evaluated
domain---and there the answer is a construction rather than a measurement: the
direct adjudicator is built from the same entitlement and outcome quantities that
define the reference label, so the two rules \emph{cannot} part where the oracle
agrees with the ledger (Section~\ref{sec:boundary2}). Whether that domain is reached
is empirical: its antecedent is an oracle that is readable, sound \emph{and}
admissible, and no system studied here supplies one
(Section~\ref{sec:admissibility}). Where it fails, substituting the direct
adjudicator makes matters worse---false proofs \GgFpDirectObserve{} against
\GgFpVthree{} on Gogs and \GlFpDirectObserve{} against \GlFpVthree{} on GitLab
(Table~\ref{tab:gogs-cross}).
(3)~\emph{Measurement (RQ3).} Can a pooled specificity over follow-up inputs provide
stable scenario-level resolution for a failure confined to a class? No: unaffected
inputs dilute it, and a class the crawl never reaches contributes no negative inputs,
so the figure is silent about that class (Proposition~\ref{prop:resolution}). The
published labels are manually verified; the limit is the denominator and the input
set.

\paragraph{What is new here, and what is not}
Two of the ingredients are established and we neither re-derive nor claim them: that
a crawl-time reachability model can go stale or under-enumerate (the predecessor
attributes its residual false alarms to crawler coverage~\cite{Mai2019Metamorphic}),
and that a reachability predicate is not an entitlement
predicate~\cite{Chaleshtari2022Metamorphic}. A third---that a pooled specificity is a
weak instrument for class-level failure---is familiar from the measurement
literature~\cite{Croft2023Data}. Section~\ref{sec:related} sets each claim beside the
prior position it extends and names the check that would count against it. What is
new is the following.
(i)~\emph{A representation argument, not a coverage observation.} The false proof is
a deterministic consequence of which of the predicate's two representations a grant
fits, and the variable that governs it is \emph{when} the model was read rather than
what was visible---which also tells staleness (repairable by recrawling) from channel
blindness (not repairable by the same recrawl;
Section~\ref{sec:realsystem}). (ii)~\emph{The alarm/policy separation, quantified.}
The technique returns an alarm; treating it as a violation is a policy we state, and
we report what four policies make of the same alarms
(Section~\ref{sec:policy}). (iii)~\emph{A conditional equivalence with a named
antecedent.} Promotion through the relation is a subset of direct adjudication once
an admissible oracle exists, which bounds the technique's added value from above; we
apply an explicit admission test that, on the systems tested, refuses it
(Section~\ref{sec:admissibility}). (iv)~\emph{A denominator argument about the
published metric.} A specificity pooled over the inputs a crawl could enumerate
cannot register a failure confined to a class that crawl did not reach
(Proposition~\ref{prop:resolution}).
Sections~\ref{sec:protocol}--\ref{sec:support} carry the evidence, which supports
these four items rather than standing as further headline claims.
