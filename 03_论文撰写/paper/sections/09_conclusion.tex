\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

For object-level authorization, \mbox{MST-wi}'s predicates do not know whether the acting
subject is entitled to the object; they know what the interface, as crawled, permitted.
Instantiating them as defined produced three findings (Section~\ref{sec:findings}).

\paragraph{Finding 1 (temporal)} As designed, the adjudication false-proofs on legitimately
granted access, deterministically (\ResObsOobVtwoFpRate{} constructed;
\ResRsMrBookkeepingFpRate{} Gitea), because an out-of-band grant fits only the wrong one of the
predicate's two representations. The alarm is the technique's own output; the count of proven
violations is a policy's, and the same \PolAlarms{} alarms yield from \PolPnoneFalse{} to
\PolPallFalse{} contradicted claims across the four policies of Table~\ref{tab:promotion}. On
the real system the mismatch appears only under the crawl-time reading
(\ResRsMismatchSnapshot{}/\ResRsMismatchDenom{}, vs.\ \ResRsMismatchLive{} live), with an
endogenous control holding final visibility fixed. Under the same frozen protocol---whose two
amendments are named and digest-bound in the second run's record---GitLab CE \GlVersion{}
commits \TmpGPolicyEvents{} transitions over \TmpGCells{} cells: \TmpGStaleFP{} stale false
proofs, all \TmpGFixed{} removed by a recrawl with the same credentials, \TmpGLiveFP{} live
false proofs and \TmpGStillMiss{} residual misses. Pooled with Gitea this is \TmpBothCells{}
cells, \TmpBothEeqY{} agreements, \TmpBothStaleFP{} stale false proofs and \TmpBothFixed{} fixes.

\paragraph{Finding 2 (oracle)} The natural repair---a readable, sound oracle that fully resolves
the proposition under test---works, and in that regime the evaluated metamorphic step provides no
incremental adjudication over a direct consumer of the same oracle and outcome. On the
constructed grid this is an identity rather than an effect, so the matched counts (false proofs
\ResAbDirectObserveFP{} vs.~\ResAbVThreeFP{}; disagreement on \ResAbDisagreementN{} of the
\ResAbNBola{} true-violation records) characterise two rules rather than measure one technique
against another. The empirical content is the antecedent---readable, sound \emph{and}
admissible---and it fails on every system studied here. Where it fails, substituting the direct
adjudicator is worse: \GgFpDirectObserve{} against \GgFpVthree{} on Gogs and \GlFpDirectObserve{}
against \GlFpVthree{} on GitLab (Table~\ref{tab:gogs-cross}).

\paragraph{Finding 3 (measurement)} The reported specificity of \ResLitReportedSpecPct{}\% is
$\mathrm{TN}/(\mathrm{TN}+\mathrm{FP})$ over the follow-up inputs the crawl could enumerate, with
manually verified labels, and that denominator cannot register a failure confined to a class the
run did not exercise.

Reading the implementation confirmed the semantics and added the mechanism---the crawl model is
frozen by a cache never invalidated, and a missing crawl defaults to ``unreachable''---and the
positive control closes the loop: on a seeded vulnerable target the surface-gated adjudicator
detects all planted instances of the fault pattern it was built for with zero false positives, and
on four seeded defect classes it detects the read bypass and nothing else. The entitlement/effect
gate is in turn checked against a real defect: on a publicly fixed object-versus-path ownership
flaw, Gitea \CveVulnVersion{} yields \CveTruePositives{} true positive with \CveFalseProofs{} false
proofs, and the fixed \CveFixedVersion{} yields \CveFixedAlarms{} alarms over \CveControls{}
control probes.

\paragraph{What a practitioner should do differently} Do not read a reachability-gated relation's
alarm as proof: because the reading time is part of the specification, an alarm raised against a
crawl-time snapshot cannot be promoted to \texttt{violation-proven} on the relation's word alone,
and the policy that decides promotion should be reported with the count it produces
(Table~\ref{tab:promotion}). If an oracle is readable, sound \emph{and} passes the independence
admission test of Section~\ref{sec:admissibility}, consult it directly rather than through the
relation; a readable, sound oracle that \emph{fails} that test does not license the substitution,
and none of the three products studied passes it. Treat a reported input-level specificity as a
corpus statistic rather than a guarantee of scenario-level resolution.

\paragraph{What a stronger claim would require} A population of real systems that \emph{contain}
the flaw, which neither the four seeded classes nor the single publicly fixed defect supplies, and
relations whose gate is not the absence of evidence. The transferable condition---the
\emph{same-observable condition}---is the one that killed our own mechanism: a component's
necessity must be tested against a substitute that consumes the \emph{same} observable quantity as
the ground truth.
