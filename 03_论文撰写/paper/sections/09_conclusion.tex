\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

For object-level authorization, \mbox{MST-wi}'s predicates do not know whether
the acting subject is entitled to the object; they know what the interface, as
crawled, permitted. Instantiating them as defined produced three boundaries. As
designed, the adjudication false-proofs on legitimately granted access,
deterministically (\ResObsOobVtwoFpRate{} constructed; \ResRsMrBookkeepingFpRate{}
Gitea), because an out-of-band grant fits only the wrong one of the predicate's
two representations. The natural repair---a readable authorization oracle---works,
and in that regime makes the metamorphic step unnecessary in our evaluated
scenarios: a single ordinary request changes nothing measurable (false proofs
\ResAbDirectObserveFP{} vs.~\ResAbVThreeFP{}; recall difference \ResAbRecallDiff{},
CI $[\ResAbRecallDiffLo{}, \ResAbRecallDiffHi{}]$; descriptive, no margin
pre-specified). On the real system the mismatch appears only under the crawl-time
reading (\ResRsMismatchSnapshot{}/\ResRsMismatchDenom{}, vs.\
\ResRsMismatchLive{} live), with an endogenous control holding final visibility
fixed: the responsible variable is time, not visibility. And the reported
specificity of \ResLitReportedSpecPct{}\% is conditional on an unstated premise
and computed in units that cannot resolve a scenario-uniform failure. Reading the
implementation confirmed the semantics and added the mechanism: the crawl model
is frozen by a cache never invalidated, and a missing crawl defaults to
``unreachable''. The positive control closes the loop: on a seeded vulnerable
target the oracle-gated adjudicator detects all planted instances with zero false
positives.

\paragraph{What we do not claim} We do not claim that \mbox{MST-wi} is wrong or
say anything about its accuracy as a detector; the native run establishes a
qualitative outcome, for one gate family, on one real system that on the tested
endpoints and transitions enforces the expected decisions. Channel proportions
are design parameters; our two MRs are two, of one family, and that is the
ceiling on everything we say; and we have not found a better detector.

\paragraph{What remains expensive} Moving from characterisation to something
stronger needs, first, a real system that \emph{contains} the flaw, so detection
can be measured; and second, relations whose gate is not the absence of
evidence---checkable against a subject's entitlements without pretending that
entitlements are reachable facts. The transferable condition is the one that
killed our own mechanism: a component's necessity must be tested against a
substitute that consumes the \emph{same} observable quantity as the ground truth;
a substitute that consumes less does not establish incremental value.
