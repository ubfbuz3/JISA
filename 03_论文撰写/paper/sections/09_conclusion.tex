\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

For object-level authorization, \mbox{MST-wi}'s predicates do not know whether
the acting subject is entitled to the object; they know what the interface, as
crawled, permitted. Instantiating them as defined produced three findings
(Section~\ref{sec:findings}).

\paragraph{Finding 1 (temporal)} As designed, the adjudication false-proofs on
legitimately granted access, deterministically (\ResObsOobVtwoFpRate{}
constructed; \ResRsMrBookkeepingFpRate{} Gitea), because an out-of-band grant fits
only the wrong one of the predicate's two representations. On the real system the
mismatch appears only under the crawl-time reading
(\ResRsMismatchSnapshot{}/\ResRsMismatchDenom{}, vs.\ \ResRsMismatchLive{} live),
with an endogenous control holding final visibility fixed: the responsible
variable is time, not visibility.

\paragraph{Finding 2 (oracle)} The natural repair---a readable, sound
authorization oracle---works, and in that regime the evaluated metamorphic step
adds no adjudication information the oracle and the request do not already carry:
a necessary condition (Lemma~\ref{lem:oracle-suff}), whose empirical face is that
a single ordinary request yields the same false-proof count (false proofs
\ResAbDirectObserveFP{} vs.~\ResAbVThreeFP{}) and the same recall (difference
\ResAbRecallDiff{}, CI $[\ResAbRecallDiffLo{}, \ResAbRecallDiffHi{}]$;
descriptive, no margin pre-specified); the one surviving difference is on recall,
where on all \ResAbDisagreementN{} disagreeing records the MR-based method fails.
On Gitea the comparison is limited to false-proof behaviour, because no
vulnerability was present on the tested surface.

\paragraph{Finding 3 (measurement)} The reported specificity of
\ResLitReportedSpecPct{}\% is not conditioned on an explicit check that the
reachability model equals the authorization state, and is computed in units that
cannot resolve a scenario-uniform failure.

Support evidence: reading the implementation confirmed the semantics and added
the mechanism---the crawl model is frozen by a cache never invalidated, and a
missing crawl defaults to ``unreachable''---and the positive control closes the
loop: on a seeded vulnerable target the oracle-gated adjudicator detects all
planted instances with zero false positives.

\paragraph{What we do not claim} We do not claim that \mbox{MST-wi} is wrong or
say anything about its accuracy as a detector; the native run establishes a
qualitative outcome, for one gate family, on one real system that on the tested
endpoints and transitions enforces the expected decisions. Channel proportions
are design parameters; our two MRs are two, of one family, and that is the
ceiling on everything we say; and we have not found a better detector.

\paragraph{What remains expensive} Moving from characterisation to something
stronger needs, first, a real system that \emph{contains} the flaw, so detection
can be measured; and second, relations whose gate is not the absence of
evidence---checkable against a subject's entitlements without pretending that
entitlements are reachable facts. The transferable condition---we call it the
\emph{same-observable necessity criterion}---is the one that killed our own
mechanism: a component's necessity must be tested against a substitute that
consumes the \emph{same} observable quantity as the ground truth; a substitute
that consumes less does not establish incremental value.
