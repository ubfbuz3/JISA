\section{Three findings}
\label{sec:boundaries}
\label{sec:findings}

The contribution is three findings, each a boundary on what the published
relations can establish about object-level authorization, and each carried by
the studies of Sections~\ref{sec:synthetic}--\ref{sec:realsystem}: a
\emph{temporal} boundary, an \emph{oracle} boundary, and a
\emph{measurement} boundary. The confirmations the findings rest
on---the predicates read at source, the published relations executed on a real
system, and an independent implementation of the same
predicates---introduce no further claim and are collected as support evidence in
Section~\ref{sec:support}.

\subsection{Finding 1 (RQ1): deterministic false proofs---the temporal boundary}

\emph{Taken as published---raw alarms, or alarms filtered by bookkeeping
exceptions---the adjudication reports a legitimate access granted out of band
after the crawl as an already-established violation, in one direction.} On the
constructed system, rate \ResObsOobVtwoFpRate{} (\ResObsOobVtwoFalseProof{} of
\ResObsOobFalseAlarm{} alarms) against \ResObsFwVtwoFalseProof{} for the same
share created in band; on Gitea, \ResRsMrBookkeepingFp{} false proofs on
\ResRsAuthorizedCells{} authorized cells (\ResRsMrBookkeepingFpRate{}) under the
crawl-time reading, against \ResRsMrBookkeepingAlarmsLive{} alarms under the live
reading---objects, entitlements and predicates held fixed, only the reading time
changed---so the error is a
property of the \emph{reading}, not of the system (Table~\ref{tab:rs-m4}). The
endogenous control (Table~\ref{tab:rs-endo}) holds final visibility fixed and
isolates the moment at which the model was read. The direction is
forced: the predicate has exactly two representations, and a grant created
outside the crawled interface has no representation but ``cannot reach''. The
predecessor attributes its residual false alarms to crawler
coverage~\cite{Mai2019Metamorphic}; we add that the failure is a function of
\emph{when} the model was read, which is what makes the Finding-2 repair
available---and empty.

\subsection{Finding 2 (RQ2): with a sound, readable oracle, the metamorphic step adds no adjudication information}
\label{sec:boundary2}

\emph{If the false proofs of Finding~1 are removed by consulting a readable
introspection surface, it is the oracle that carries the work: for the
authorization relations and scenarios evaluated here, replacing the entire
metamorphic step with a single ordinary request---one that consumes the same
readable oracle and access outcome---provides no additional measurable detection
benefit.}

We state the reason rather than present it as a discovered effect, because it is
a necessary condition on the claim, not an empirical regularity.

\begin{lemma}[oracle-sufficient adjudication]
\label{lem:oracle-suff}
Let $O$ be an authorization oracle that is \emph{readable} (its answer can be
obtained) and \emph{sound} (its answer equals the true permission), and let $R$
be the observed outcome of an ordinary request. Any adjudicator whose verdict is
a function $V = f(O,R)$ of $O$ and $R$ alone is extensionally equal to the
direct consumer $D := f(O,R)$: the two return the same verdict on every input.
Consequently a metamorphic step can be \emph{shown} to be necessary only by
exhibiting an observable it consumes that the direct consumer does not.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
An adjudicator of the stated form and $D$ are the same
function applied to the same pair of arguments, so they agree pointwise; the
last sentence is the contrapositive.
\end{proof}

The lemma concerns \emph{adjudication} information only. It does not imply that a
metamorphic relation cannot improve input generation, search-space reduction, or
execution efficiency: a relation may guide which requests are issued, or which
gated subset is exercised, while carrying no verdict information beyond
$(O,R)$. It is a requirement on \emph{claims}, not a verdict on metamorphic
testing: it says only that a verdict computable from $(O,R)$ cannot be shown to
beat the direct consumer of $(O,R)$. The evaluated relations do consult a third
quantity---the crawl-time reachability model $M$---so the empirical question is
whether $M$ carries authorization information that a readable, sound $O$ does not
already supply. For such an $O$, $M$ is either concordant with the true state, in
which case it adds no information $O$ lacks, or discordant, in which case it is
false by construction (Finding~1). Source inspection confirms the two relations
read only $M$ and the response (Section~\ref{sec:impl}); the experiment confirms
the consequence.

Evidence: Table~\ref{tab:ablation}---\ResAbDirectObserveFP{} false
proofs vs.~\ResAbVThreeFP{} (a tie at zero, not a win), recall
\ResAbDirectObserveRec{} vs.~\ResAbVThreeRec{}, difference \ResAbRecallDiff{},
95\% CI $[\ResAbRecallDiffLo{}, \ResAbRecallDiffHi{}]$---descriptive, not a
test; no margin was fixed in advance. Gitea independently reproduces the
zero-false-proof result (\ResRsDirectObserveFp{} vs.~\ResRsVThreeFp{}), but does
not provide a recall comparison because the tested surface contains no seeded
vulnerability; the cross-vendor systems (\S\ref{sec:gogs}) and the seeded control
(\S\ref{sec:seeded}) complete the evidence. Two qualifications are part of the
claim: the surviving
difference is on recall alone, and there the MR-based method fails on all
\ResAbDisagreementN{} disagreements; and the control is \emph{constructed}, not
discovered---\texttt{direct\_observe} instantiates exactly the $D$ of the lemma,
so the tie is the empirical face of the necessary condition, a statement about
the definitions, not a coincidental measurement.

\subsection{Finding 3 (RQ3): the reported specificity lacks scenario-level resolution}

\emph{The reported \ResLitReportedSpecPct{}\% is a statement about a corpus of
generated inputs; it is not conditioned on an explicit check that the
reachability model coincides with the true authorization state, and its
denominator cannot register a scenario-uniform failure.} The omission:
adjudication is a function of the
reachability model, so where model and true state diverge---the out-of-band case
of Finding~1---the figure measures agreement with the tool's own crawler; the
authors concede the mapping is ``not always
available''~\cite{Chaleshtari2022Metamorphic}, but the number is reported
without the condition. The denominator: a failure uniform across a scenario
class contributes only as many false positives as that class emits inputs, and
is diluted by the rest of the run (Proposition~\ref{prop:resolution}: in our
approximation the configuration that misreports a whole class of legitimate
access still scores \ResSpecRowOneSpecPct{}\%; Corollary~\ref{cor:resolution}:
no fixed threshold can guarantee detection of such a class). We are not saying
the figure is false or the false positives misdiagnosed; the claim is that such a
metric cannot discharge its own premise nor see a class-uniform failure.

\subsection{Admissibility, and falsification}
\label{sec:admissibility}

Three properties must be kept apart. A surface is \emph{readable} when its answer
can be obtained from the vantage in question; \emph{sound} when its answer equals
the true authorization state; and \emph{admissible}, for promotion to
\texttt{violation-proven}, when it is readable \emph{and} one can give a reason
that it is not of the same kind as the defect under test---not itself a
reachability artefact whose blind spot correlates with the blindness tested.
Readability is what makes soundness usable, but it neither implies soundness nor
confers admissibility: the owner-vantage surface is readable on every cell in
each system (\ResRsCellsSnapshot{} Gitea, \GgCells{} Gogs, \GlCells{} GitLab),
yet sound on Gitea alone---it agrees with actual access on \GtOracleAgree{},
\GgOracleAgree{} and \GlOracleAgree{} of those cells respectively. The
cross-vendor systems are therefore not cases of ``no oracle'': a readable
membership surface exists on both, but it is unsound for the authorization
channels exercised here, so it fails clause~(2). Clause~2 is a per-system
judgement and not automatable from readability alone; the cross-vendor results
make it concrete (Gogs and GitLab fail it; their adjudicators convert the blind
spots into \GgFpVthree{} and \GlFpVthree{} false proofs). Where the condition
holds, the honest method consumes the oracle directly and drops the relation;
where it does not, the honest method abstains---as v3 does on the unreadable
stratum, where the aggregate table would have ranked it best, a ranking that
reflects abstention rather than improved detection. Each finding would fall as
follows: Finding~1, if the predicates were defined over live rather than
recorded reachability; Finding~2, by an authorization MR needing an observable
that a succeeding request plus a readable oracle cannot produce; Finding~3, by
a specificity in scenario units, or with the condition explicit, that still fails
to separate the configurations studied here.
