\section{Threats to validity}
\label{sec:threats}

\paragraph{Source versus execution} We read the predicates at source (built under JDK~8 and
Maven; Section~\ref{sec:impl}) and then executed the relations, unmodified apart from the build
configuration and our harness (Section~\ref{sec:native-run}). The harness substitutes an HTTP
observation channel---exact for the reachability predicate, not exercised for the content
predicate, whose invocation count we report rather than claim cleanliness we did not
demonstrate. The run converts one class of claim, that a false proof is produced by the
\emph{published} relations on a real system under the promotion policy $P$; it does not convert
the rest: the scenario/configuration and cell studies remain statements about our instantiation,
and we report no sensitivity or specificity attributable to \mbox{MST-wi}.

\paragraph{Metric and statistics} Records within a design cell are not independent and the
records are a fixed enumeration, so we report counts and rates rather than inferential
intervals, and no result rests on statistical significance. The RQ2 ablation is paired and is
reported as a discordance count. Finding~3 is a statement about what the published denominator
pools over, not about the value of any specificity figure: no conclusion here depends on it.

\paragraph{Two MRs, one family} We instantiated \ResDesignMrs{} MRs of one family---relations
gated on the absence of evidence. The native run lifts the count to \NtMrExecuted{} of
\ResArtAuthzMrTotal{} catalog authorization relations but not the family: only the
reachability-gated ones alarm in our run. Widening the family requires relations gated
otherwise, which is a different experiment.

\paragraph{Real systems} Three systems (one lineage pair, one independent), chosen for
accessibility, none vulnerable on the tested surface, so only the false-proof side is measured,
within one domain, the git forge. Channel proportions are topology design parameters, not field
prevalence, and three systems tested once each are still not a population. Every statement is
tied to the tested releases (Gitea \ResRsVersion{}, Gogs \GgVersion{}, GitLab \GlVersion{}):
the permission interfaces and enumeration behaviour we rely on are version-specific, so a
change in a release requires revalidation rather than re-reading our counts.

\paragraph{Stratification and the references used in it} The split of Section~\ref{sec:stratum}
is on whether the introspection surface returned a reading for the record, not on the
configuration switch: \MiiFlagOnButUnavailable{} records sit in configurations that enable
introspection but whose probe returns nothing. The two oracle-free references there are
deliberately weak---one unconditional, one guessing from a single request---because the point is
to size the metamorphic step against methods that need nothing, not to identify the best
oracle-free detector; a differential prober across accounts consumes more than those references
do and is instantiated in Section~\ref{sec:baselines} instead. Both share an observable with the
labelling rule, as \texttt{direct\_observe} does, so only the differences against them are meant
to be read.
